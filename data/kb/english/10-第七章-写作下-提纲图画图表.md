## 第三节　提纲、命题、图画、图表类作文

### 一、提纲类作文

#### （一）作文类型

提纲类作文主要有以下六种类型。

**1. 说明介绍型**

此类作文一般要求考生对某个人物、事件、季节、节日等进行简单介绍，描述其典型特征、相关活动、意义价值等。说明介绍型的行文结构见图 7-2。

【图】图 7-2　说明介绍型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「引出介绍对象，并合理表达看法。」
- 「中间段」→「具体描述人物特征、事件经过、季节特点、节日活动等。」
- 「末段」→「总结文章或表达感情。」

**2. 观点论证型**

此类作文一般要求考生根据题目所给的论点，按照题目要求并通过举实例、列数据、讲道理等方式论证或反驳论点。观点论证型的行文结构见图 7-3。

【图】图 7-3　观点论证型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「提出一种观点。」
- 「中间段」→「通过举例、引用数据、阐述道理等方式论证或反驳该观点。」
- 「末段」→「表明或重申自己的观点，表明做法、态度或提出建议。」

**3. 对比选择型**

此类作文一般要求考生比较关于某事物、现象或问题的两种观点或两种做法，并表明自己对该事物、现象或问题的态度或观点。对比选择型的行文结构见图 7-4。

【出处：库课英语教材 书p266 / PDF p276】

【图】图 7-4　对比选择型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「引出要论述的事物、现象或问题。」
- 「中间段」→「先论述第一种观点或做法及其理由，再论述另一种观点或做法及其理由。」
- 「末段」→「表明自己的态度或观点，并陈述理由。」

**4. 问题解决型**

此类作文一般要求考生从题目的提示文字入手，指出相关问题，提出解决方法或应对措施。问题解决型的行文结构见图 7-5。

【图】图 7-5　问题解决型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「指出存在的问题，说明问题的危害或影响。」
- 「中间段」→「提出解决问题的具体方法或措施。」
- 「末段」→「表明自己的态度或做法，并展望前景或提出建议。」

**5. 利弊评述型**

此类作文一般要求考生客观地分析和评论某事物或某现象的正反两方面。考生并不需要表明态度，判断对与错，尤其是对有较大争议的话题，可采用平衡利弊的态度，提出建议或设想。利弊评述型的行文结构见图 7-6。

【图】图 7-6　利弊评述型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「描述要分析和评论的事物或现象。」
- 「中间段」→「论述利弊两方面的观点。」
- 「末段」→「陈述自己的观点，提出建议或设想。」

**6. 现象阐释型**

此类作文一般要求考生对某一现象进行解释或说明，分析其产生的原因或带来的影响，并加以评论。现象阐释型的行文结构见图 7-7。

【图】图 7-7　现象阐释型的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「描述现象，并进行简单的解释或说明。」
- 「中间段」→「分析这种现象产生的原因或可能带来的影响。」
- 「末段」→「针对这种现象进行评论，表明自己的观点或提出建议。」

【例 6】Directions: For this part, you are required to write a composition on the topic **The Season I Like Best**. You should base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

（1）表明自己最喜欢的季节；
（2）介绍自己喜欢这个季节的原因；
（3）表达自己的喜爱。

**[写作思路]**

| 段落 | 写作要点 | 具体内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 首段 | 引出介绍对象 | "我"最喜欢的季节是夏季 |
| 中间段 | 介绍喜欢这个季节的原因 | 假期长，有很多有趣的事情可以做，包括去露营、去海滩和游泳 |
|  |  | 可以吃到各种多汁的水果，喝到各种冰镇饮料 |
|  |  | 阳光明媚、光线充足，让"我"感到快乐且充满活力；每天都能穿 T 恤衫和短裤，享受自由自在的感觉 |
| 末段 | 总结全文，表达喜爱 | 夏季是"我"最喜欢的季节，是玩得痛快和享受生活的时间 |

**[范文]**

**The Season I Like Best**

When it comes to the season, I like summer best.

Firstly, I have a long summer vacation every year, so I don't have to worry about having no time to have fun. There are lots of interesting things for me to do in summer, including going camping, going to the beach and swimming in pools. Secondly, summer is a delicious season when I can have all kinds of juicy fruits and iced drinks. Thirdly, it is always sunny and bright in summer, which makes me feel happy and energetic. I can wear my favorite T-shirts and shorts every day. This feeling of ease and freedom is something only summer can bring.

In summary, summer is without doubt my favorite season. It is the time to have fun and enjoy life. I do like it best.

【例 7】Directions: For this part, you are required to write a composition on the topic **The Necessity of Reducing Screen Time**. You should base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

（1）大学生有必要减少屏幕时间；
（2）分析过长的屏幕时间造成的危害；
（3）重申观点。

**[写作思路]**

| 段落 | 写作要点 | 具体内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 首段 | 提出观点 | 大学生应该意识到减少屏幕时间的必要性 |
| 中间段 | 分析过长的屏幕时间造成的危害 | 影响学业 |
|  |  | 导致视力下降甚至越来越糟 |
|  |  | 导致不良的心理健康状况 |
| 末段 | 重申观点，提出建议 | 大学生应该尽量减少屏幕时间，将时间用于获取知识和培养能力 |

**[范文]**

**The Necessity of Reducing Screen Time**

It is widely accepted that college students should realize the necessity of reducing screen time. As we all know, long screen time does great harm to them.

There are lots of reasons to support this argument. First, spending too much time on electronic devices will surely affect their studies. Second, due to long screen time, their eyesight declines and even goes from bad to worse. What's more, a growing body of research has shown that long screen time is also linked to poor mental health, including increased anxiety and social isolation.

From what has been discussed above, college students should try their best to reduce screen time. I suggest that they should spend their limited time at university on more important matters such as acquiring knowledge and developing abilities.

【例 8】Directions: For this part, you are required to write a composition on the topic **The Real Goal of Education**. You should base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

（1）很多人认为教育只是为了让人们将来更好地就业，其实不然；
（2）教育的目的还应在道德和正义层面指引人们；
（3）你的观点。

**[写作思路]**

| 段落 | 写作要点 | 具体内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 首段 | 反驳固有观点，提出新观点 | 很多人认为教育的目的是只让人们将来更好地就业，但教育的目的还应在道德和正义层面指引人们 |
| 中间段 | 论证新观点 | 几乎没有接受过道德教育的人无法过上真正有意义的生活 |
|  |  | 缺乏道德和正义的社会将是一片混乱 |
|  |  | 道德与正义层面的指引能让个体成为"完整的社会人" |
| 末段 | 总结观点，表明态度 | 教育的真正目的是教书和育人，两者并重，缺一不可 |

**[范文]**

**The Real Goal of Education**

In the modern world, many people hold that the goal of education is only to make people better prepared for their future careers. They believe that education should offer the knowledge and skills needed for a certain profession. However, many facts show that the goal of education should also lie in its guidance for people on morality and justice. The arguments for this point of view can be listed as follows.

First of all, a man who rarely receives moral education can't live a really meaningful life. Furthermore, a society without morality or justice will be in disorder. Lastly, guidance on morality and justice enables individuals to become "complete social beings".

I hold the opinion that the real goal of education is the combination of imparting knowledge and educating people. Therefore, we should pay attention to both equally.

【例 9】Directions: For this part, you are required to write a composition on the topic **Living on or off Campus**. You should base your composition on the outline given in Chinese below.

（1）有人认为，大学生应该住在校内；
（2）有人认为，大学生住在校外更好；
（3）你的观点。

**[写作思路]**

| 段落 | 写作要点 | 具体内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 首段 | 开门见山，点明主题 | 对于大学生应该住在校内还是校外，不同的人有不同的观点 |
| 中间段 | 论述观点，列举理由 | 应该住在校内：校园能为学生提供安全的生活环境和良好的学习氛围；有机会与其他学生建立深厚的友谊 |
|  |  | 住在校外更好：独立生活能保护隐私；可以自由安排自己的学习时间，避免来自同伴的各种干扰 |
| 末段 | 表明自己的观点及理由 | 大学生应该住在校内，掌握人际交往的技巧对他们未来的发展非常重要 |

**[范文]**

**Living on or off Campus**

When asked whether college students should live on or off campus, different people have different views.

Some people think that college students should live on campus. In their opinion, the campus can provide students with a safe living environment and a good learning atmosphere. Besides, living on campus will give them a chance to build deep friendships with other students. However, others hold that living off campus is better. They maintain that living independently can protect students' privacy. In addition, it allows them to arrange their study time freely and avoid various distractions from their peers.

Weighing up the two views, I am for the former one. By living on campus, college students can master the skills of interpersonal communication, which is of great importance to their future development.

**How to Prevent Traffic Jams**

In addition, methods should be adopted to improve the efficiency of traffic control.

As for me, I will work as a volunteer to help direct traffic. I will also advise people around me to use public transportation. Undoubtedly, it is essential that everyone get involved in the efforts to reduce traffic jams and make our daily life more convenient.

### 二、命题类作文

### 四、图表类作文

#### （一）出题形式

图表类作文一般会给出一个或几个统计表格、曲线图、条形图或饼状图，有时还会以英文或中文提纲的形式给出提示。考生写图表类作文时，应仔细阅读题目要求，分析图表给出的数据信息，筛选出有效的数据，提炼主题，并列举相关论据来支撑主题。

#### （二）行文结构

考生写图表类作文时可采用三段论的形式，其行文结构见图 7-11。

【图】图 7-11　图表类作文的行文结构：纵向三栏流程图，左侧为三个方框（首段、中间段、末段），各自用横线连到右侧对应的说明方框。
- 「首段」→「描述图表数据，概述图表传达的信息。」
- 「中间段」→「指出图表反映的问题或趋势，并分析其产生的原因或可能带来的影响。」
- 「末段」→「总结全文，提出建议或展望未来。」

| 英文模板 | 中文译文 |
| --- | --- |
| As is shown/indicated by the figures in the table/graph/bar chart/pie chart, ______（图表数据）. | 如表格/曲线图/条形图/饼状图中的数字所示，______。 |

**Leisure Activities Among City Residents**

As is shown by the figures in the pie chart, when it comes to leisure activities, 30% of city residents choose to watch movies and TV shows, 25% read books and magazines, another 25% participate in social activities, and only 20% do outdoor sports.

There are some reasons for the above preferences. Firstly, lots of residents prefer to watch movies and TV shows because they help residents forget the troubles and stress in their daily lives. Secondly, reading and participating in social activities are popular because the former helps residents learn more knowledge and the latter allows them to maintain connections with others. Finally, due to various constraints such as bad weather, busy schedules and lack of sports facilities, fewer residents choose outdoor sports.

As far as I am concerned, the ways city residents spend their spare time show they have diverse interests. It is advisable that the government should pay attention to this and provide support by building more public libraries, gyms and community centers.

In real life, some well-known figures have never been educated in a university. But they still made great achievements through diligence or remarkable talent. Based on this fact, some people draw the conclusion that university education is useless.

在现实生活中，有些知名人士从未接受过大学教育。但他们仍然凭借勤奋或非凡的才华取得了卓越的成就。基于这一事实，一些人得出结论：大学教育是无用的。
